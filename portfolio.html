Founder of a 300-volunteer global nonprofit, trainer to youth across ten districts, and a machine learning intern who still finds time to fix the wiring on a fire detector.

I grew up between two disciplines that most people keep separate: community organizing and hands-on engineering. In school that meant building an automatic night light one term and running nutrition sessions for rural schools the next. That habit never left — I still move between spreadsheets of volunteer retention data and lines of Python.

Since 2025 I've led NOVA Nourish Foundation from an idea into a working organization across 15+ countries, trained youth volunteers for JAAGO Foundation, and represented Bangladesh's young people at the UN's CSW70. This year I added a machine learning internship to the mix — because the same instinct that made me chase field data on 100+ adolescent girls' nutrition now makes me chase clean training data too.
